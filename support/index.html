Help with movie night

Getting started

How does MovieMatch work?

Everyone in the screening swipes the same lineup of movies from your library. Tap I'm in or Skip. When every person says I'm in to the same movie, it's a match, and that's tonight's film.

Connect Plex

Open Settings → Sign in with Plex, approve in the browser, then pick your server. You can also enter a server address and token by hand under "Manual setup".

Connect Jellyfin

Open Settings → Jellyfin, enter your server address (for example http://192.168.1.20:8096 or your public address), your username and password. Your password is only used to sign in and isn't stored; the app keeps an access token in the Keychain.

Can I try it without a server?

Yes. Without a server, MovieMatch uses a set of demo movies with simulated friends so you can see how it works.

Away from home

It works at home but not on cellular

MovieMatch talks to your own media server, so the server must be reachable from outside your home. For Plex, turn on Remote Access in your server's settings. MovieMatch automatically switches between your server's home and remote addresses. For Jellyfin, use an address that works outside your network, such as a reverse proxy or VPN.

Group screenings

Swipe with friends

Start a FaceTime call, choose SharePlay as the session type, then tap Start the show. Everyone on the call who has MovieMatch can join from the SharePlay banner.

Play the match

Open the match and tap Play on Plex or Play on Jellyfin to open the movie on your server.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

Disconnect your server in Settings, swipe to delete past screenings, or delete the app to remove everything. See the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck?

Email universe@kportal.net and describe what happened, including your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't include your server address, password or token.
