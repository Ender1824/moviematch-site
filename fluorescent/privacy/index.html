Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

The short version: Fluorescent has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers that receive your data. Everything the game knows stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, your saved run, your journal of notes and tapes, and your best times.
  • Which suit finishes you own (a copy of what the App Store reports, so they still show with no network).

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What the game uses on your iPhone

  • Motion sensors, so that turning the phone looks around and walking in place moves you. Motion data is used as you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Headphone motion, only if you turn on head tracking in Settings, so that sound follows your head. Also never stored or sent.
  • Local network and Bluetooth, only if you start or join a co-op game, to find players near you. Co-op is device to device. The other players' phones receive the name of your device, your suit's colour and finish, and where you are in the game. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

The game does not use the camera, the microphone or your location.

Purchases

Suit finishes are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the game only which finishes you own. The developer never sees your payment details. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no personal data, usage data or identifiers from the game. We don't sell or share data, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Children

Fluorescent is a horror game and is not aimed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
