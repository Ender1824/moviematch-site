How do I move and look?

Use the stick at the bottom left to walk; push it all the way to run. Turn the phone to look around, or swipe anywhere on the screen. Double-tap to crouch, flick down to slide, and hold the middle of the screen for two seconds to recenter the view. You can also turn the stick off in Settings and walk in place instead.

The view drifts or points the wrong way

Hold the middle of the screen for two seconds to recenter. If you'd rather not use motion at all, turn off "Turn phone to look" in Settings and swipe to look.

It is too dark, or too frightening

Settings has a brightness calibration and a Scare intensity slider. There is also a Reduced motion switch if the camera shake is uncomfortable.

What are the keys, breakers and codes?

Each level tells you what it wants when it starts, and the counters under the top bar show what is left. Keys glow gold. Breakers stand on a post with an amber light; throwing one is loud. Some doors want a three-digit code, found on two torn notes.

Co-op doesn't find the other player

Both phones need Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on, and both need to allow Local Network access for Fluorescent (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). One player hosts; the others join.

I bought a suit finish and it isn't there

On the title screen, open Suits and tap Restore purchases. Purchases follow your Apple Account, so use the same account you bought with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
