Fluorescent

Carpet. Wallpaper. The hum.

A found-footage horror game for iPhone. You are in a hazmat suit with a camcorder, somewhere that goes on for ever: a yellow office, a tiled pool hall, service corridors, baths, a street of little houses under a ceiling. Find the keys, restore the power, and find the way down. Something tall lives here, and it listens for your footsteps.

  • Turn your phone to look, or swipe. Walk with the stick, or by walking in place.
  • Crouch, hide, and keep quiet. Running is loud.
  • Play alone, or with people nearby over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
