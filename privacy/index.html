Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version: MovieMatch has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone, on your own Plex or Jellyfin server, and with the friends you invite.

What stays on your device

  • The name and emoji you choose for screenings.
  • Your lineup filters (genres, moods, decades, runtime, rating).
  • Past screenings: the picks your group matched on, whether you watched them, and the star ratings you entered.
  • Your media server's address and access token. The token is kept in the iOS Keychain and is never shown, logged or shared.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can also disconnect a server in Settings, or delete individual past screenings.

Who the app talks to

  • Plex (plex.tv), only if you choose "Sign in with Plex", to sign you in and find your servers. This is covered by Plex's privacy policy.
  • Your own Plex or Jellyfin server, to read your movie library, posters and "watched" status.
  • Apple SharePlay, when you start a group screening on a FaceTime call. Your name, emoji, votes and the lineup's movie details are shared with the people on that call, through Apple. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • YouTube, only when you tap "Find trailer", which opens a YouTube search for the movie.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no personal data, usage data, crash reports with personal data, or identifiers from the app. We don't sell or share data, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Children

MovieMatch doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
